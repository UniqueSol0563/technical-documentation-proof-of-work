## 02 — Public Workflow

## Objective

To demonstrate a structured, security-conscious, and human-governed method for transforming fragmented opportunity information into a traceable qualification decision.

The workflow establishes clear inputs, controlled processing stages, review points, expected outputs, and improvement actions without disclosing protected decision logic or private framework components.

## Purpose

This document presents a sanitized view of a human-governed workflow for managing employment, contract, and project opportunities.

It demonstrates how fragmented information can be transformed into an organized, reviewable, and traceable decision process while preserving human authority.

## Workflow Summary

`Capture → Organize → Review → Qualify → Approve → Track → Improve`

| Stage | Public purpose | Controlled result |
|---|---|---|
| Capture | Collect approved opportunity information | Structured intake |
| Organize | Arrange relevant information consistently | Review-ready record |
| Review | Check accuracy, completeness, privacy, and risk | Validated information |
| Qualify | Compare the opportunity with approved criteria | Supported recommendation |
| Approve | Record the authorized human decision | Approved next action |
| Track | Preserve status and follow-up | Traceable record |
| Improve | Evaluate results and feedback | Approved improvement |

### Human-Governed Control

AI may assist with organizing, summarizing, comparing, and documenting approved information.

The human user defines the outcome, establishes the boundaries, evaluates uncertainty, makes consequential decisions, authorizes external actions, and stops or redirects the workflow when necessary.

Detailed review controls and stopping conditions remain protected.

### Professional Relevance

This workflow demonstrates the ability to:

- Transform fragmented information into structured documentation
- Analyze workflows and information movement
- Separate AI assistance from human decision authority
- Establish review points and operating controls
- Preserve decision context and traceability
- Identify privacy, security, and operational risks
- Incorporate verified feedback into process improvement

These capabilities can support technical documentation, systems analysis, AI governance, cybersecurity, information governance, knowledge management, quality review, and process improvement.

### Control and Disclosure Boundary

This public document shows the workflow structure and professional value.

It does not disclose private records, proprietary prompts, internal scoring criteria, complete decision logic, master templates, detailed operating rules, protected framework architecture, or the full GENE1SIS™ operating ecosystem.
