## 04 — Qualification Matrix

### Objective

To demonstrate how approved public criteria can be applied consistently to a sanitized opportunity record while documenting evidence, missing information, and the need for human review.

### Purpose

This document evaluates the fictional opportunity record created in Component 03.

The matrix separates confirmed alignment, partial alignment, missing evidence, and unresolved information. It supports a controlled recommendation without replacing human judgment or exposing private decision factors.

### Disclosure Boundary

This public matrix uses sanitized information and public evaluation criteria. It does not disclose proprietary scoring weights, complete decision logic, private qualifications, personal circumstances, or protected framework architecture.

## Public Status Key

The matrix uses descriptive statuses instead of private numerical scores.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Confirmed | The available record directly supports the criterion. |
| Partial | Some supporting information is present, but evidence is incomplete. |
| Missing | Required information is not available in the record. |
| Human review required | A person must interpret the evidence or apply a private decision factor. |
| Not applicable | The criterion cannot be applied to this fictional demonstration. |

## Public Qualification Matrix

| Qualification criterion | Available evidence | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Role and function clarity | The opportunity identifies technical documentation and AI-assisted content review responsibilities. | The general function is understandable. | Confirmed |
| Work arrangement | The record states remote work within the United States. | Compatibility depends on the individual’s approved work-location requirements. | Human review required |
| Engagement type | The record identifies the opportunity as contract work. | The engagement category is clear, but the duration is missing. | Partial |
| Compensation suitability | An hourly range is described, but the exact range is withheld from the public example. | Suitability cannot be determined from the sanitized record. | Human review required |
| Skills alignment | The source describes information analysis, quality evaluation, documentation, instruction review, security awareness, and stakeholder collaboration. | General capabilities are identifiable, but complete qualification requirements are missing. | Partial |
| Application readiness | The deadline, verified contact, complete requirements, and reporting structure are unavailable. | The record is not ready for an application decision. | Missing |
| Source verification | The source is explicitly identified as fictional and created for demonstration. | It cannot be treated as a verified live opportunity. | Not applicable |

## Matrix Interpretation

The seven public criteria produced:

- 1 Confirmed status
- 2 Partial statuses
- 1 Missing status
- 2 Human review required statuses
- 1 Not applicable status

These results are not a numerical score and do not indicate that an applicant is unqualified. They show that the fictional opportunity record lacks enough verified information to support a final qualification or application decision.

## Controlled Recommendation

**Recommendation:** Hold

**Reason:** The general function and some responsibilities are identifiable, but the source is fictional and important opportunity information remains missing or unsuitable for public evaluation.

**Approved next action:** Preserve the matrix as evidence of the qualification-review process and carry the Hold decision into Component 05 — Project Results and Lessons.

**Stopping condition:** No application, external communication, or consequential action is authorized from this fictional qualification record.
