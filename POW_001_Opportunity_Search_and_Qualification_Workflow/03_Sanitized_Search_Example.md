## 03 — Sanitized Search Example

## Objective

To demonstrate how fragmented opportunity information can be captured, sanitized, verified, and converted into a structured record for controlled qualification review.

## Purpose

This document provides a realistic public example of the information-handling stages described in the POW_001 Public Workflow.

The example demonstrates source review, information organization, security-conscious sanitization, requirements extraction, and preparation for human evaluation.

## Disclosure Boundary

This example uses fictionalized and sanitized information. It contains no private account information, credentials, personal identifiers, confidential communications, or protected internal decision logic.

## Expected Result

A structured, source-aware, and review-ready opportunity record that can proceed to qualification without exposing sensitive information or bypassing human judgment.

## Fictional Source Fragment

> **Opportunity:** Technical Documentation and AI Quality Specialist  
> **Organization:** Northstar Systems Group — fictional organization  
> **Work arrangement:** Remote — United States  
> **Engagement type:** Contract  
> **Compensation:** Stated hourly range  
> **Summary:** Review AI-assisted content, identify unclear or incomplete information, document findings, and collaborate with project stakeholders to improve quality, consistency, security awareness, and instruction alignment.

**Source status:** Demonstration source created for this sanitized public example. It is not a live job posting.

## Initial Information Review

The source fragment contains enough public information to begin organization, but additional details would be required before an application decision.

**Information present:**

- Opportunity title
- General work arrangement
- Engagement type
- Compensation category
- Primary responsibilities
- Quality and documentation expectations

**Information not provided:**

- Exact contract duration
- Complete qualification requirements
- Application deadline
- Benefits or equipment terms
- Reporting structure
- Verified employer contact information

## Sanitization Actions

Before publication, the example was reviewed to:

- Remove personal identifiers and private account information
- Exclude credentials and confidential communications
- Replace any real organization with a fictional name
- Withhold exact compensation and private decision factors
- Separate confirmed information from missing information
- Preserve enough context to demonstrate professional capability
