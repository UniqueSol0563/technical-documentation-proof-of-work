# 06 — Professional Application and Transfer Value

## Purpose

This document explains how the skills demonstrated in POW_007 transfer into professional work involving AI evaluation, technical documentation, systems analysis, governance, quality assurance, and risk-aware decision support.

The goal is to show how the project can be understood by employers, clients, reviewers, and other stakeholders.

---

## Professional Problem Addressed

Organizations increasingly use AI-generated content to support:

- research,
- customer communication,
- documentation,
- analysis,
- decision support,
- workflow automation,
- and internal operations.

The challenge is not only generating AI output.

The challenge is determining whether that output is:

- aligned with the intended task,
- complete,
- reliable,
- supported,
- usable,
- and appropriate for human approval.

This proof-of-work demonstrates a structured approach to that review problem.

---

## Transferable Professional Capabilities

### 1. AI Response Evaluation

The project demonstrates the ability to review AI-generated content against defined requirements rather than relying on surface quality alone.

Transfer value:

- AI evaluator roles
- response-quality review
- model-output assessment
- human-in-the-loop operations
- annotation and quality-control work
