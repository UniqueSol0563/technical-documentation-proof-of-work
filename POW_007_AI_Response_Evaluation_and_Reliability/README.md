# POW_007 — AI Response Evaluation and Reliability

**Status:** In Development  
**Version:** v0.1  
**Date:** September 10, 2026

## Purpose

This proof-of-work demonstrates a structured, human-reviewed approach for evaluating AI-generated responses for clarity, relevance, reliability, and usability.

The project focuses on identifying where an AI response may sound correct but still fail to fully meet the user's actual need.

## What This Project Demonstrates

- AI response evaluation
- Systems analysis
- Human-in-the-loop review
- Technical documentation
- Process improvement
- Reliability and quality assessment
- Controlled disclosure of internal methods

## Disclosure Boundary

This public proof-of-work shows selected evaluation steps, sanitized examples, and professional results.

It does not disclose proprietary prompts, complete internal decision logic, private scoring methods, protected framework architecture, or the full internal operating framework.

## Human and AI Contribution

**Human-Directed | AI-Assisted | Human-Reviewed**

AI tools may support information processing, drafting, comparison, and organization.

Final evaluation, judgment, approval, and publication remain under human control.
